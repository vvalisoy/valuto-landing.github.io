Safer by design

Scams that simply can’t happen

Paying by QR doesn’t just skip the fees. No card, no PIN, and no cash also means the oldest travel scams have nothing to work with.

  • ATM & card skimming

    No magnetic strip or chip ever goes into a machine — so a tampered ATM or card terminal has nothing to clone.

  • Shoulder surfing

    You never type your card PIN on a shop keypad where bystanders or a hidden camera can watch. Every payment is approved on your own phone.

  • Data breach theft

    The merchant only receives a payment confirmation — never your card number or banking details. If their system leaks, nothing of yours is in it.

  • Cash short-changing

    You pay the exact amount on the screen, in their currency. No notes handed over, no change counted back wrong on purpose.

No. Valuto works with the QR codes they already accept.

What does it cost?

Around 2% all-in — roughly a third of what a typical bank card costs abroad. You’ll always see the exact amount before you confirm.
